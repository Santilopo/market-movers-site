## 5. Course curriculum (6 modules, 41 lessons)

1. **Tools & setup:** TradingView, TradeSea, Hyperliquid (outside US), Tradeify, Lucid Trading, futures contracts & tick values, eval rules & drawdown. Explains how each tool and prop firm works.
2. **How the market really moves:** candlesticks, highs & lows, trends, liquidity, imbalances, inversion fair value gaps (IFVG), break of structure (BOS), microeconomics, market ecology, market conditions, time asymmetry, indicators.
3. **Risk management & psychology:** position sizing, daily rules, and the mindset to follow them.
4. **Strategy 1: The eval strategy:** account types, consistency rule, account withdrawals, daily withdrawals, eval mindset. All the confluences combined into a strategy built around prop firm rules.
5. **Strategy 2: The live account strategy:** trading your own capital.
6. **Past lives & trade breakdowns:** recorded sessions and real trades, wins and losses.

A detailed lesson-by-lesson plan (41 lessons with what to cover and who leads) exists as a separate doc.
